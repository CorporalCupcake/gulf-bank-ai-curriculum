Gulf Bank AI Curriculum

A concept-first path from deep foundations to the current frontier, built for arriving at a Gulf bank ready to lead conversations. Each topic will become an interactive lesson focused on intuition, worked banking examples, how ideas connect, and what the latest research says.

Every lesson follows this shape

  1. The intuition in plain words, around one concrete banking example
  2. How it really works, with the maths where it earns its place
  3. How it connects to neighbouring topics, shown interactively
  4. Where the field is now, with dated, cited research
  5. How it fails, and what bad AI-generated work on it looks like
  6. What to say in the room: a 30-second executive version and the deep version
  7. Self-check questions

Assumptions

Written before the country and role were confirmed, so regulation covers both the UAE and Saudi Arabia, and depth is pitched at a general AI, ML and data science role. Tiers will be re-ranked once those are known.
